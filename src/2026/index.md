FMOz will be held at the University of Queensland, 4th and 5th of June, 2026.

### Schedule

The program can be found on the [**program**](program.html) page.
Attendance is free, however please RSVP by email so we have estimates for room bookings.

The program runs from **10am-5:15pm** on the 4th, and **9am-4:30pm** on
the 5th, to allow those travelling interstate to fly in on the morning. On
both the 4th and 5th we will take a group lunch at [Saint Lucy's][lunch] on campus.
There will be a dinner on the 4th at [Viet De Lites][dinner] in South Bank, a ferry ride away.

[lunch]: https://www.saintlucy.com.au/
[dinner]: https://www.visitsouthbank.com.au/places/viet-de-lites-ec7df

While attendance is free, unfortunately, we do not have funding to provide catering.

### Location

FMOz 2026 will be located at [The University of
Queensland St Lucia Campus](https://campuses.uq.edu.au/st-lucia) in Brisbane.
The workshop will be held at **Room 442** in the [Andrew Liveris Building (46)][andrew].

[andrew]: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/833712366#map=17/-27.499545/153.014263&layers=V

UQ can be reached by City Cat (ferry) to UQ Lakes, the Brisbane Metro (bus) from South Bank or the CBD, or
bus to Chancellors Place bus stop from the West.
[Translink Journey Planner](https://jp.translink.com.au/) can be used to plan public transport.

FMOz falls in the study break, so paid parking on campus will likely be available,
the closest being along Sir William Macgregor Drive or [Conifer Knoll
carpark](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/48015757#map=19/-27.499386/153.018104).
