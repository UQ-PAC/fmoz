FMOz will be held at the University of Queensland, 4th and 5th of June, 2026.

The program can be found on the [program](/fmoz/2026/program.html) page. Attendence is
free, however please RSVP by email so we have estimates for room bookings.


### Presentations

Talk selection is non-competitive as far as possible and we do our utmost to
provide time to everyone who wishes to present. Presentations may be complete
work, or work in progress, tool demonstration or tutorials you believe would be
of interest to the audience. 

Proceedings of the workshop are not published but abstracts will be uploaded to
the website as have been in previous years.

#### Format

Presentations time-slots will be approximately 30-45 minutes, depending on the
number of speakers.
